Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Stay Mate app ("Application") for mobile devices created by Khac Thien Nguyen ("Service Provider") as a free service. This service is provided “AS IS”.


Information We Collect and Use

For the purpose of delivering the main features of the Application, we may collect:

  • Account information: name, phone number, email, and user ID.
  • Contract information: rental contract details provided by your landlord or property manager.
  • Uploaded images: photos you upload to report issues or view contract images.
  • Chat messages: messages exchanged between you and your landlord.
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, and crash logs (only for stability and debugging).

The Application does not collect precise location information from your device.


How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the core functionalities of the Application, including:

  • Managing your rental account
  • Displaying contract and housing information
  • Supporting communication between tenants and landlords
  • Handling issue reports with uploaded photos
  • Improving app stability and user experience

Third-Party Access

We do not sell or share your personal data for marketing purposes.

We may share data with:

  • Your landlord or property manager for rental-related interactions
  • Trusted service providers that assist us in app functionality (e.g., cloud storage)
  • Government authorities only when required by law

Permissions

The Application may request access to:

  • Camera: to capture images when reporting issues
  • Photo Library: to upload or download contract images or chat attachments

We only access images that you explicitly choose.


Data Retention

We retain your data as long as you use the Application or as long as required to provide the service. You may request deletion by contacting us at: staymate.home@gmail.com.


Children

The Application is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can remove it.


Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information.


Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the Application means you accept the updated policy.
