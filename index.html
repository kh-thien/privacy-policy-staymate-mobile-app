Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Stay Mate app ("Application") for mobile devices created by Khac Thien Nguyen ("Service Provider") as a free service. This service is provided "AS IS".

Stay Mate is a rental property management application designed exclusively for tenants. It allows you to manage your rental information, communicate with your landlord, track bills, report issues, and access contract documents.

Information We Collect and Use

For the purpose of delivering the main features of the Application, we may collect and process the following information:

Account Information

  • Name, phone number, email address, and user ID
  • Profile information you provide

Rental Contract Information

  • Rental contract details provided by your landlord or property manager
  • Contract images you upload or that are shared with you
  • Contract terms, rental period, and property address

Bill and Payment Information

  • Monthly rental bill details (amount, due date, payment status)
  • Landlord's bank account information (for display purposes only)
  • Note: The Application does NOT process payments. It only displays payment information. All actual bank transfers must be completed through your own banking app.

Communication Data

  • Chat messages exchanged between you and your landlord
  • Images and attachments sent via chat
  • Message timestamps and read status

Issue Reports and Maintenance Requests

  • Descriptions of reported issues or maintenance requests
  • Photos you upload when reporting problems
  • Issue status and resolution history

Images and Media

  • Photos you capture using the camera for issue reports or chat messages
  • Images you select from your photo library to upload
  • Contract images you view or download

Device and Technical Information

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version and crash logs (for stability and debugging only)
  • Push notification tokens (if you enable notifications)

The Application does not collect precise location information from your device.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the core functionalities of the Application, including:

  • Managing your rental account and profile
  • Displaying and allowing you to download contract documents and images
  • Showing bill information and payment details for manual bank transfers
  • Facilitating communication between you and your landlord through in-app chat
  • Processing and tracking issue reports with uploaded photos
  • Sending notifications about new messages, bills, or issue updates (if enabled)
  • Improving app stability, performance, and user experience

Data Storage

Your information is stored securely using cloud storage services. Images and documents are stored on secure servers and are accessible only to authorized parties (you and your landlord).

Third-Party Access

We do not sell or share your personal data for marketing purposes.

We may share data with:

  • Your landlord or property manager: for rental-related interactions, communication, contract management, and issue resolution
  • Cloud service providers: trusted third parties that assist us in app functionality (e.g., cloud storage, messaging infrastructure)
  • Government authorities: only when required by law or legal process

App Permissions

The Application may request the following permissions:

  • Camera: to capture photos when reporting issues or sending images in chat
  • Photo Library: to select and upload images for issue reports, chat messages, or to download contract images
  • Notifications: to send you alerts about new messages, bills, or issue updates (optional, can be disabled in device settings)
  • Internet Access: to sync data, send messages, and communicate with servers

We only access images and data that you explicitly choose to share or upload.

Payment Processing

Important: Stay Mate does NOT handle or process any financial transactions. The Application only displays:

  • Bill amounts and due dates
  • Landlord's bank account information for reference

All actual payment transfers must be completed by you through your own banking application. We do not have access to your bank account, credit card information, or any financial credentials.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as:

  • You actively use the Application
  • It is required to provide the service
  • Required by law or contractual obligations

You may request data deletion by contacting us at: staymate.home@gmail.com. Upon request, we will delete your personal data within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required by law.

Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at: staymate.home@gmail.com

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes through the Application or via email. Continued use of the Application after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
